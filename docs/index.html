How to Interpret EMG Push Task Metrics

RMS (Muscular Activity)

Root Mean Square reflects the electrical amplitude and motor unit recruitment intensity of the muscle fibers. Higher values signify greater neuromuscular effort. In push tasks, Triceps Brachii typically exhibits substantial recruitment.

MDF (Median Frequency & Fatigue)

Calculated via Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) Power Spectral Density (10–500 Hz). A continuous downward shift (negative slope) in MDF over the 180-second trial is the gold standard indicator of localized muscle fatigue.

Co-Contraction Ratio (Triceps/Biceps)

Ratio of agonist (Triceps) to antagonist/stabilizer (Biceps) activity. Reflects joint stiffness, biomechanical coordination, and push efficiency under varied isometric resistance.
